Total Soccer: Mundial

Privacy Policy for Total Soccer: Mundial

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Total Soccer: Mundial respects your privacy.

The game does not require user registration and does not provide user accounts.

Information We Collect

Total Soccer: Mundial does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

The game does not collect:

  • names, email addresses, phone numbers, or postal addresses;
  • precise or approximate location;
  • contacts;
  • photos, videos, or audio;
  • financial or payment information;
  • advertising identifiers;
  • device identifiers for tracking purposes;
  • health or fitness information.

Local Game Data

Certain game information may be stored locally on your device, such as:

  • game settings;
  • selected language;
  • tournament progress and saved tournaments;
  • gameplay-related state.

This information is used only to provide game functionality and is not transmitted by Total Soccer: Mundial to a developer-operated external server.

Analytics and Advertising

Total Soccer: Mundial does not use advertising SDKs, analytics services, behavioral tracking, or advertising profiling.

The game does not display third-party advertisements.

Accounts and Payments

Total Soccer: Mundial does not provide user accounts, login functionality, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or other payment functionality.

Network Access and Android System Services

The Android version of Total Soccer: Mundial runs using Android System WebView and the Capacitor platform.

The game itself does not operate a backend service and does not send gameplay or personal information to the developer.

Android and Android System WebView may independently process limited technical or diagnostic information as part of the operating system and WebView infrastructure. Such processing is controlled by Google and/or the device manufacturer and is subject to their respective privacy policies and device settings.

WebView usage metrics have been explicitly disabled for Total Soccer: Mundial.

Android cloud backup has also been disabled for the application. Depending on the Android version and device manufacturer, the operating system may still support device-to-device transfer of application data as part of a user-initiated device migration.

External Links

The game may contain links that open external websites or services.

When you leave the game and visit an external service, that service may process information according to its own privacy policy.

Total Soccer: Mundial does not transmit personal information to those services as part of opening such links.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because Total Soccer: Mundial does not maintain user accounts or store personal information on developer-operated servers, the developer does not retain personal user data.

Locally stored game data can be removed by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the game.

Children's Privacy

Total Soccer: Mundial does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Since the game does not transmit or store personal information on developer-operated servers, no personal user database is maintained by the developer.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality of Total Soccer: Mundial changes or if applicable legal or platform requirements change.

The updated version will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.
